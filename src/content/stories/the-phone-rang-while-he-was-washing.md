The phone rang while Daniel was washing the dishes. He's been accumulating dishes all week so that he could wash them on friday night while listening to his favourite podcast. That was his usual routine. He looked at the phone screen and he saw his mom's name on it. He dried his hands and tried to answer fast before she'd hang up. "Hey mom," he said. "How are you?" "I'm good," she said. "We were just having dinner and everyone came. Only you were missing. What are you going to do tonight? "I'll probably go to see some friends later," he said even though he knew he wouldn't. He didn't like lying to his mom, but he also didn't want her to worry. As a kid he showed a lot of promise and he was always afraid of ruining his reputation. "I'm happy for you. You always make friends so easily everywhere you go," his mom said. "You should have seen Elton tonight. He went crazy and ran after a little nut all over the house. It was so cute. I'll send you some pictures later." "I'd love that. Thanks," he replied. "I heard your friend David got married last week. Was he mad you didn't go to his wedding? You used to be such good friends when you were young. I remember how I once found the two of you giving some young kitties a bath. I was horrified. You almost drowned them to death! Do you remember it Daniel? "I think you told me that story a couple of times," he said. "It was too complicated for me to go to the wedding. I wish I could."

It's been 5 years since Daniel moved to Spain. At first he just knew he had to go to another country. Then it became a habit and he just didn't think he could ever come back. Everytime he went to Israel for a visit he felt repulsed and his body got tense. There was something about this place that closed down on him. In spain, on the other hand, he felt free. He felt light. Maybe even too light. Sometimes he'd get vertigo from too much freedom. He had this thought in mind that in Madrid no one bothers him, and that if he'd died it'll take at least a couple of days until someone would notice. Israel was like a ball and chain tied to his ankle. Spain was like walking on the moon. When he first landed in Madrid he didn't know even one person in the city. It excited and terrified him at the same time.

"Well we miss you. I hope you're happy with your firends over there. Shabat Shalom. I don't want to interrupt you no more," his mom said and hung up the phone before he had the chance to say anything. He looked at his bookshelves and thought well, I wasn't really lying about spending time with my friends. He learned to be self-suficient and filled his time with music and books. He thought of his first days in Madrid and how enregetic he was. He remembered that indian guy that hosted him for the first couple of days until he found an apartment. Everything was exciting back then. "Well Daniel. Tonight we're gonna show you Madrid. You won't forget this night." The indian guy said to him while he was heating some sort of ketamin on a piece of tin foil and then inhaled the fumes with a little transparent tube. Daniel has never seen anything similar before. They were in a car with another guy from México who told them about his plan to smuggle cocaine into Spain and become rich. "Come try some of this shit Daniel. It'll make you feel good. I promise," said the mexican guy. They were in a rented Audi A8 that the mexican guy paid for with money he borrowed from the bank. He never paid the debt. "Ok let's roll," the mexican guy said. "Let's take this beautiful slab for a ride. But first I need a bit more of this magic powder. The fumes nocked him out and it took him about two minutes to come back. "Wow that was good. Now we're good to go," he said. Daniel was somehow ok with the situation. He thought it might just be cultural differences. The mexican guy got arrested a year afterwards, and the indian guy went back to India after his visa expired. Daniel hasn't seen them for many years.
