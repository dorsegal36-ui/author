"Wake up mom. We need to hide." It's six am on saturday and hear the sound of a missile alert. "Mom, I think there's a missile alert. Let's move," I say, but I'm not sure what to do. The last time I heard this kind of alert was more than fifteen years ago. I was a child back then and there was a big war with Lebanon; and now that I hear this sound again, my body reacts automatically like a well trained dog. "I'm coming, I'm coming," she says. "What is it. What's happening?"

She asks, but her body already knows and she's dressing up in no time. "Where's dad? Where are we going?" She asks while following me. "Dad went out for a walk half an hour ago," I say. I don't know where I'm taking her, but after a couple of seconds we're at the building staircase with the rest of the neighbours. It's not our apartment. I came to Israel for a short visit and my brother left me his place for a couple of days. Then I invited my parents for the weekend. "Who's shooting at us this time?"

One old lady asks. "I heard it’s the Iranians. Could be a nuclear bomb," another neighbour jokes. I see two frightened kids cuddled with their mom right next to my feet. An old lady in a sleeping gown holding her little chihuahua in her trembling hands. Then we hear some huge explosions and the building shakes. "Do you know where did dad go to?" My mom asks. "He said he goes to the beach, but I'm sure he'll be fine. He's a big boy and he's probably found a better shelter than us," I say with a little smile.
