He never had a good reason to go to Hungary in his life, but when his friend Julian told him he’s getting married to a Hungarian woman he didn’t think twice and loved the idea of going to a new country and meeting new people, and inevitably he also thought about the opportunities he might have of meeting girls. He always saw in every trip the possibility of getting to know someone in such a way that his mind used to go there instead of trying to enjoy the moment.
